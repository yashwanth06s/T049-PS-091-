1. Domain Glossary

TermDefinition & Domain Significance
PublisherThe digital property supplying ad slots. Sets a floor CPM reserve price.
AdvertiserBrand purchasing ad space. Holds either a PREPAID wallet or CREDIT account.
CampaignAn ad initiative with targeting, flight dates, bid model, and daily budget.
CPMCost Per Mille (per 1,000 views). Billed immediately on impression delivery.
CPCCost Per Click. Billed only when an impression receives a verified click.
eCPMEffective CPM: Standardised revenue per 1,000 impressions: eCPM = CPC * CTR * 1000.
Floor PriceReserve CPM set by publisher. Requests clear at or above this price.
Second-Price AuctionHighest eligible bidder wins; clears at max(2nd_eCPM, Floor) + Rs 0.01. Single bidder pays Floor.
Frequency CapMax 3 impressions per user per calendar day and max 1 per clock hour.
Budget PacingSpreads daily budget across 24 hourly buckets matching the traffic profile.
Parity ThrottlingWhen OVER_PACE (≥120%), campaign sits out on odd request sequence numbers.
Invalid Traffic (IVT)>5 clicks from same user on same campaign within 10 minutes marks all burst clicks INVALID.
ClawbackPublisher share credited on invalid clicks is clawed back from monthly payout.
Payout RolloverMonthly payouts under Rs 5,000 roll forward to the subsequent month.

2. Business Rules Register (BR-01 to BR-12)

Rule IDRule NameOperational Logic & Formulas
BR-01Eligibility & TargetingMatches geo, device, category. Request time within flight dates. Daily spend < daily budget. Prepaid wallet ≥ 1 max bid; Credit non-suspended.
BR-02Frequency CappingMax 3 impressions/user/day (calendar day IST) and max 1 impression/user/hour (clock hour IST).
BR-03Bid NormalisationCPM: eCPM = max_bid. CPC: eCPM = max_bid * trailing_CTR * 1000. Trailing CTR default = 0.5% if <1000 impressions; else valid_clicks / impressions.
BR-04Second-Price AuctionWinner clears at max(second_eCPM, floor) + 0.01. Single bidder pays floor. Unfilled if all bids < floor.
BR-05Auction Tie-BreakEqual eCPM bids won by campaign with lower cumulative spend today, then campaign_id ascending.
BR-06Budget PacingHourly budget buckets. OVER_PACE if spend ≥ 120% of schedule. In UNDER_PACE, catch-up allows up to 150% of current hour bucket.
BR-07Pacing ThrottlingWhen OVER_PACE, campaign bids only on even request sequence numbers (seq_no % 2 == 0).
BR-08Billing MechanicsCPM billed per impression at clearing CPM / 1000. CPC billed on click at max_bid capped at daily budget + 1 bid.
BR-09Wallet & Credit LimitsPrepaid debits wallet to 0. Credit balance may go down to -credit_limit. Any breaching charge stands and suspends account.
BR-10IVT Detection & Clawback>5 clicks in 10-minute rolling window marks all INVALID. Advertiser refunded; publisher share clawed back.
BR-11Publisher Revenue Share70% share if monthly billed > Rs 1,00,000; else 60%. Payouts < Rs 5,000 roll to next month.
BR-12Flight End & ExpiryCampaigns serve until 23:59:59 on flight_end. Unspent budget lapses and never carries forward.
